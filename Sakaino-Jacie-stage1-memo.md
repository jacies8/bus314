## Approach

Describe the methodology: what data you will use, what ratio categories you will analyze, and how the results will be structured. Keep the language clear so non-technical readers can follow the plan.

To get a complete view of ARQT's health, six categories will be covered.
- Performance Ratios: measures growth and assesses market valuation.
- Profitability Ratios: measures how profitable the company is through gross margin ratios and net profit margin ratios.
- Efficiency Ratios: measures how well inventory and assets are being uses through asset turnover ratios and sales outstanding ratios.
- Leverage Ratios: measures how much debt the company is using to finance growth through the debt to equity ratio.
- Liquidity Ratios: measures the ability to meet debts and costs using liquid assets through the current ratio or quick ratio.
- Du Pont Analysis: measures and breaks down the company's return on equity into profitability, efficiency, and levaerage.

I will be using data from the SEC EDGAR database as well as earning releases from the Arcutis website. There are also websites like Yahoo Finance and Bloomberg that provide analyses of the company.
